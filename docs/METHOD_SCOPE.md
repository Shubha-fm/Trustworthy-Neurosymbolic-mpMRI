# Method and assurance scope

- The five classes are experimental imaging categories, not clinical diagnoses.
- The symbolic priors are imaging-informed computational priors, not clinical rules.
- Formal SMT verification applies to the fusion head over sound latent abstractions.
- The complete voxel-to-output network is not directly SMT-verified.
- TLA+ verifies release-workflow logic, not classifier accuracy.
- Split conformal prediction quantifies predictive uncertainty and is not a formal proof.
- The current architecture assumes all four MRI sequences are available.
